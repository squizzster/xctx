- The package-install smoke is part of the default full pytest suite and skips instead of downloading dependencies unless network use is explicit.
- `make package-install-smoke` runs the package-install smoke with its explicit online dependency-install allowance.
- Local skills and reports are development aids; the current code, tests, and loaded YAML are the source of truth when they drift.
